# Surface: home page (/)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: a school administrator deciding on a founding pilot, and a teacher who opens the page each morning to present today's devotional without signing in. Action: present today's devotional (working, on the page); secondary: become a founding school. Proof: the real day's devotional, presentable; the same Scripture at two reading levels (labelled sample). Constraints: no invented customers, prices or content; WCAG 2.2 AA; works on Chromebooks and phones.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a well-made school hymnal, a numbered book for every school day. It refuses the SaaS landing page (cream ground, centred hero, card grid, dark CTA band) and the soft "cream, serif, terracotta" rendition of a book.

OWN-WORLD: Hymnal bookcloth navy carries whole regions (masthead and closing "back cover"), with gilt stamping for the wordmark and rules. Inside, the page is bright thin Bible paper, ink black, and red rubrics: red italic instructions, red verse and day numbers. Caslon throughout for reading and display; system sans only for controls. Square corners, printed hairline and double rules, no cards, no shadows, no icon tiles.

STORY: A teacher sees today's devotional numbered like a hymn and presents it in one click. An administrator reads the order of the day with its rubrics, sees one passage set for four ages, and writes to become a founding school.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A navy cloth masthead with the gilt wordmark and the navigation. Below it on paper, a hymnal page: a large red day number ("No. 14", or "Today") at the top left beside the devotional's title and a running head (reference · date), then the working inline presenter as the page's verse block, with Present full screen as the primary action. One line of pitch and the founding link sit in the right margin like a hymn's tune and meter notes; they stack below on phones.

FORM: User-pinned hymnal and letterpress direction (first of three offered). seed: none (pinned): "Hymnal & letterpress (Recommended)": "Like a well-made hymnal or study Bible: warm off-white paper, true ink black, one deep brick-red accent for headings and verse numbers (the old red-letter tradition), a classic book typeface with small capitals, thin printed rules instead of cards. 'Day 14 of 180' set like a hymn number." Rendered from the world's full range: bookcloth fields and gilt, not cream.
