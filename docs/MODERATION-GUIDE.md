# Community moderation guide

**Do not open any interactive feature until this guide's readiness list is real.** The software enforces the checkable parts (a human moderator exists; the covenant is required; the sign-offs are recorded). It cannot check that your people are ready. That is yours to ensure.

## Before launch

| Requirement | How it is met |
|---|---|
| At least one designated human moderator, ideally two | Grant the Moderator role (Admin → Staff). Automatic check on Admin → Launch |
| Published covenant | `/community/covenant`; must be accepted before taking part. Wording approved by your pastoral, legal and school leaders. Sign-off "covenant reviewed" |
| Working reporting tools | Report on any post; queue under Community → Moderation. Test on staging |
| Tested moderation controls | On staging, with the moderators: report, hide, restore, lock replies, suspend, appeal. Sign-off "moderation tested" |
| Safety escalation procedure | Written, with names and phone numbers. Sign-off "escalation procedure" |
| Response expectations | Written and shared. Sign-off "response expectations" |
| Privacy guidance | This section and docs/PRIVACY-RETENTION.md. Sign-off "privacy guidance" |
| Suspension procedure | Below. Sign-off "suspension procedure" |

**Suggested starting expectations** (for you to adopt or change, not decided by the software): urgent safety reports reviewed the same day by someone who can act; ordinary reports within two working days; appeals decided within thirty days by someone other than the original moderator. If your team cannot meet these, keep the features off or narrow.

## Principles

1. Scripture and care for the person come first; moderate to protect people, not to win arguments.
2. Prefer the smallest action that works: a private word, then hide, then restrict, then suspend.
3. Never reveal who reported.
4. Act on content, not on assumptions about the person.
5. Write the reason every time. The reason is shown to the person and kept in the record.

## Handling a report

1. Open Community → Moderation. Reports show a snapshot of what was reported, so later edits do not hide it.
2. Read the context. Decide: dismiss, hide, or escalate.
3. If hiding: choose a reason; the author sees a notice in the app (email is not sent in this release).
4. For repeat or serious problems, restrict or suspend (below).
5. Close the report. It stays in the record until retention clears the quoted text.

## Prayers

Prayer requests are tender. Hide only for the covenant's reasons: identifying a student, private details about a third party, harmful content. Contact the author privately where your procedure allows. Never mock or minimize a request. Private prayers are meant for their author alone; do not go looking for them.

## Urgent safety concerns and escalation

If a post suggests someone may be harmed, or involves a child's safety:

1. Hide the content if that reduces harm, but **do not delete** the record.
2. Escalate to the named person in your escalation procedure **immediately by phone**, not through the app. The app does not page anyone.
3. Follow your school's and your jurisdiction's mandatory-reporting duties. Obtain legal advice; the software does not know your law.
4. Record what was done, when and by whom.

## Suspension procedure

1. Use a restriction (time-limited suspension, or a ban) from the moderation screens; give a reason the person will see.
2. The person can still sign in to read the notice and file an appeal; they cannot post.
3. A different moderator hears the appeal.
4. Lift the restriction when the period ends or the appeal succeeds.
5. Subscriptions are not changed automatically; decide your billing policy in advance.

## Groups

Group owners moderate their groups and can remove members; platform moderators can act on reports. New groups may require review before opening (default on). Review each: purpose, guidelines, owner, and that it is for adult educators.

## Looking after moderators

Moderating prayers and conflict is heavy. Work in pairs where possible, rotate, and have someone to talk to.
