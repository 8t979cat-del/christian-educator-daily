# Educator onboarding guide

Written for the educators in a pilot school. The same material is in the app at **/welcome**, which new educators see right after setting up their profile.

## 1. Joining

1. Your school contact gives you an **access code** (like `ABCD-EFGH-JKMN`). Keep it to yourself; it is for your school.
2. Create an account and verify your email.
3. Finish the short profile.
4. Open **Pilot** in the app (or follow the link your school sent) and enter the code.

You will see how many days the pilot runs. Access ends on that date by itself. **Nothing is charged**, and nothing asks for a card.

## 2. A day in the app

1. **Read** today's devotional: Scripture, a short reflection, a prayer, a question for discussion and a practical classroom idea. Grade-level labels help you choose.
2. **Present** it: open classroom presentation mode for large, calm type on a projector. Students never need accounts.
3. **Save** what you like with bookmarks; **search** the library by topic, passage or grade.
4. **Use resources** for lesson ideas and printable material.

## 3. Talking and praying together (when switched on)

Your school's pilot team switches these on gradually, so you may not see all of them on day one.

- **Scripture-centered reflections.** Public discussions are attached to approved Scripture and devotional content: you reflect on that passage. There are no free-standing public posts, no feed that ranks people, and no popularity counts.
- **Prayer contributions.** You can share a prayer request with your name, anonymously, or keep it private to yourself.
- **Prayer acknowledgements.** Others can quietly say they prayed. It is a kindness, not a score.
- **Private educator groups.** Original posts are allowed only inside authorized private groups, for adult educators, with a stated purpose and guidelines. Group content is visible only to members and the group's moderators.

## 4. Community guidelines

Before taking part you accept a short covenant (read it any time at Community → Covenant). In short: be kind and honest, stay with the Scripture, protect the people you teach, and report concerns. Reports go to human moderators.

## 5. Privacy expectations

- **Never** post student names, photos or anything that could identify a student, in a prayer request, a reflection or a group. The platform checks for names, but you are the first safeguard.
- Do not share private details about colleagues or families.
- Private prayers are visible only to you.
- Pilot reports show **totals** for the school and the pilot. No one is ranked or scored, and no one's faith is measured.
- There are no direct messages in this release, and students have no community accounts.

## 6. If something is wrong

- **Report a post:** use the report option on it.
- **Report a bug or share an idea:** the Feedback link at the top of every page. It notes only which page you were on.
- **An urgent safety concern about a person:** contact your school's usual safeguarding contact first. The app records and routes reports, but it does not page anyone.

## 7. For school administrators

1. Hand out codes personally (not in group chats). Ask us for more or for a different expiry.
2. Watch seats on your school page; they fill as people join.
3. Decide with your head of school who may start groups.
4. Tell your staff what you expect about student privacy.
5. Send feedback early and often. Tell us what you would pay for, honestly, including "nothing".
6. Know the end date, and what happens then: access stops; nothing is billed.
