# Future development backlog

Ordered by what most reduces risk for the pilot first. None of this is promised.

## Before or alongside the pilot (should)

1. Terms of use, privacy notice and consent screen for adults (needs counsel). **Blocks real users.**
2. Real devotional content: authors, theological review, fourteen days scheduled, licensed Scripture.
3. Custom SMTP, restore rehearsal and error-collector verification on staging.
4. Browser end-to-end tests of the signed-in flows against a staging backend (Playwright against staging).
5. Self-service data export and account deletion.
6. Email notices for moderation actions and appeals.
7. A paging or notification route for urgent reports (to a named human).

## Soon after (could)

8. Printable lesson handouts and a weekly planning view.
9. Accessible slide-style presentation extras: font and contrast controls remembered per device.
10. School administrator dashboard of their own school's totals (not only platform-wide).
11. Pilot invitation emails sent from the app, with explicit review before sending.
12. Pilot data export (totals) to a spreadsheet for the weekly review.
13. Per-seat self-service for school administrators.
14. Install-to-home-screen and offline reading of today's devotional.

## Later, only if the pilot shows need

15. Additional Bible translations and parallel reading.
16. Grade-band variants of a devotional.
17. Audio reading of the devotional.
18. Group tools for professional learning communities.
19. A parent-facing read-only summary (needs its own privacy design).

## Deliberately out of scope

Student accounts; direct messaging; public free-standing posts; algorithmic feeds; leaderboards or any spiritual scoring; public sharing of identifiable student information.
